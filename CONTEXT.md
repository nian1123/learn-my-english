# English Video Learning

A personal learning environment for developing American English listening and vocabulary through sentence-level practice with authentic interview videos.

## Language

**Study Video**:
An English interview video selected by the learner for focused listening practice.
_Avoid_: Imported video, lesson video

**Study Library**:
The learner's locally retained collection of Study Videos, ordered around resuming recent practice.
_Avoid_: Video catalog, content feed

**Caption Source**:
The English timed text from which Learning Sentences are derived. It may be manually authored, automatically generated, or supplied by the learner.
_Avoid_: Transcript provider, subtitle file

**Learning Sentence**:
The primary practice unit: a complete spoken sentence bounded by punctuation and meaningful pauses, and associated with the matching interval in a Study Video.
_Avoid_: Subtitle fragment, caption line

**Local Revision**:
A learner-authored correction to a Learning Sentence's text, boundaries, or timing that leaves the original Caption Source unchanged and recoverable.
_Avoid_: Corrected source caption, overwritten subtitle

**Word Lookup**:
A contextual explanation opened from a word or phrase in a Learning Sentence. English is shown by default; Chinese is optional and hidden by default.
_Avoid_: Translation popup, word translation

**Word Bank**:
The learner's saved contextual Word Lookups, retaining the originating Learning Sentence even if its Study Video later leaves the Study Library.
_Avoid_: Dictionary history, vocabulary catalog

**Listening Practice**:
Repeatedly listening to a Learning Sentence with playback speed and transcript visibility controls, without recording or pronunciation scoring.
_Avoid_: Speaking assessment, pronunciation test
